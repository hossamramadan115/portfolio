About Me

Building mobile apps people actually enjoy using

I'm Hossam Ramadan, a Flutter developer focused on building modern, fast, and user-friendly mobile apps for iOS and Android from a single codebase.

I design apps with Clean Architecture and Bloc/Cubit state management, so they stay scalable, testable, and easy to maintain. My work includes real-time tracking, Firebase push notifications, Google Maps, and secure payment integrations, and I've shipped apps to both the App Store and Google Play.

I enjoy turning ideas into polished products, paying attention to the details, and constantly learning new technologies.
